# Customer Support - Billing & Payment FAQs

## Receipts and Documentation

### Question: Where can I find my receipt?
**Answer:** You can find your receipts in several ways:
- Log into your account and go to "Order History" or "Billing"
- Check your email inbox for the confirmation email sent after purchase
- Download PDF receipts from the "Documents" section in your account
- Contact support with your order number for a receipt copy

### Question: How do I download my invoice?
**Answer:** To download your invoice:
1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to "Billing" or "Invoices" section
3. Select the invoice you need
4. Click "Download PDF" button
5. Invoices are also automatically emailed to your registered email address

### Question: Can I get a duplicate receipt?
**Answer:** Yes, you can get duplicate receipts:
- Access your account's order history
- Select the relevant transaction
- Click "View/Download Receipt"
- If you need assistance, contact support with your order number and date

## Payment and Billing

### Question: What payment methods do you accept?
**Answer:** Accepted payment methods:
- Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Gift cards and store credit
- Bank transfers for large orders

### Question: Is my payment information secure?
**Answer:** Yes, your payment is secure:
- We use industry-standard SSL encryption
- Payment data is processed by certified payment processors
- We are PCI DSS compliant
- Card details are never stored on our servers
- Two-factor authentication available for additional security

### Question: Why was my payment declined?
**Answer:** Common reasons for declined payments:
- Insufficient funds in your account
- Incorrect card details entered
- Card expired or not activated
- Billing address doesn't match card records
- Bank fraud prevention blocking the transaction
- Card issuer restrictions
- Try a different payment method or contact your bank

### Question: How do I update my payment method?
**Answer:** To update your payment information:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Payment Methods" or "Billing Settings"
3. Click "Add Payment Method" or "Edit" next to existing method
4. Enter new card details or select a different payment option
5. Save changes
6. Your new payment method will be available for future purchases

### Question: Can I split payment between multiple methods?
**Answer:** Split payment options:
- Some orders can be split between gift cards and credit cards
- Apply gift card balance first, then charge remaining to card
- Multiple credit cards typically not supported in one transaction
- Contact support for special payment arrangements on large orders

### Question: How do I view my billing history?
**Answer:** To access your billing history:
1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to "Billing" or "Payment History"
3. View all past transactions with dates and amounts
4. Download or print statements as needed
5. Filter by date range or transaction type
6. Export to CSV for your records

### Question: What should I do if I was charged incorrectly?
**Answer:** If you notice an incorrect charge:
1. Check your order confirmation email for the correct amount
2. Verify the charge in your account's billing history
3. Contact our billing support within 30 days
4. Provide order number and details of the discrepancy
5. Include screenshots if possible
6. We'll investigate and issue a refund if an error occurred
7. Refunds typically process within 5-7 business days

### Question: Can I get a refund to a different payment method?
**Answer:** Refund policy for payment methods:
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method used
- Cannot redirect refunds to a different card or account
- If original card is closed, refund may be returned by your bank
- Contact your bank if you don't receive refund within expected timeframe
- Store credit may be offered as an alternative in some cases

## Promotions and Discounts

### Question: How do I use a promo code?
**Answer:** To apply a promo code:
1. Add items to your cart
2. Proceed to checkout
3. Look for "Promo Code" or "Discount Code" field
4. Enter your code exactly as provided
5. Click "Apply"
6. The discount will be reflected in your order total
7. Promo codes cannot be combined unless stated

### Question: Why isn't my promo code working?
**Answer:** Common reasons:
- Code has expired
- Minimum purchase requirement not met
- Code only applies to specific products
- Code already used (one-time use codes)
- Typo in the code entry
- Code not valid for your region
- Cannot be combined with current sale items

### Question: Can I apply a promo code after placing an order?
**Answer:** Promo code policy:
- Promo codes must be applied at checkout before completing purchase
- Cannot be added after order is placed
- If you forgot to use a code, contact support immediately
- We may be able to apply the discount if order hasn't shipped
- Refund of difference may be issued in some cases
- No retroactive discounts after order has shipped

### Question: How do I get promotional emails?
**Answer:** To receive promotions:
1. Scroll to the bottom of our website
2. Enter your email in the newsletter signup box
3. Click "Subscribe"
4. Check your email for a confirmation link
5. You can also opt-in during account creation
6. Manage preferences in your account settings

## Refunds and Credits

### Question: When will I receive my refund?
**Answer:** Refund timeline:
- Returns are processed within 2-3 business days of receipt
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Credit cards: 5-7 business days
- Debit cards: 7-10 business days
- PayPal: 3-5 business days
- You'll receive an email confirmation once the refund is processed

### Question: How do I check my refund status?
**Answer:** To check your refund:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Order History" or "Returns"
3. Find the returned order
4. Check the refund status
5. Email confirmation sent when refund is processed
6. Contact support if it's been longer than expected timeframe

### Question: What is store credit and how do I use it?
**Answer:** Store credit information:
- Store credit is account balance you can use for purchases
- Issued for returns, promotional offers, or compensation
- View balance in your account under "Store Credit" or "Account Balance"
- Automatically applied at checkout
- Cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash
- No expiration date unless specified

### Question: Can I get a refund for a promotional item?
**Answer:** Promotional item refunds:
- Free promotional items are typically non-refundable
- If returning an order that included a free item, you may need to return it
- Bonus items received with purchase should be returned with main item
- Contact support for specific promotional refund policies
- Store credit may be offered for certain promotional returns

## Subscriptions and Recurring Billing

### Question: How do I manage my subscription?
**Answer:** To manage subscriptions:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Subscriptions" or "Manage Subscriptions"
3. View all active subscriptions
4. Update payment method, frequency, or delivery address
5. Pause or cancel subscription
6. Changes take effect on next billing cycle

### Question: How do I cancel my subscription?
**Answer:** To cancel a subscription:
1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to "Subscriptions"
3. Select the subscription you want to cancel
4. Click "Cancel Subscription"
5. Confirm cancellation
6. You'll retain access until the end of current billing period
7. Confirmation email will be sent

### Question: Why was I charged for a subscription I cancelled?
**Answer:** If you were charged after cancellation:
- Check the cancellation date vs. billing cycle date
- Subscription remains active until end of paid period
- Next billing may have already been processed
- Contact billing support with subscription details
- Refund may be issued if charge was made in error
- Confirm cancellation in your account settings

### Question: Can I change my subscription billing frequency?
**Answer:** Changing billing frequency:
1. Go to your subscription settings
2. Select "Edit Subscription"
3. Choose new billing frequency (monthly, quarterly, annual)
4. Changes apply to next billing cycle
5. Prorated adjustments may apply
6. Save changes and receive confirmation

## Taxes and Fees

### Question: Why was I charged sales tax?
**Answer:** Sales tax information:
- Sales tax is charged based on your shipping address
- Required by law in states/countries where we have business presence
- Tax rate varies by location
- Calculated automatically at checkout
- Displayed before you complete purchase
- Tax exempt? Contact support with exemption certificate

### Question: What are the shipping fees?
**Answer:** Shipping fee structure:
- Standard Shipping: $5.99 (or free over $50)
- Express Shipping: $12.99
- Next Day: $19.99
- International: Varies by destination
- Fees shown during checkout
- Free shipping promotions available periodically

### Question: Are there any hidden fees?
**Answer:** Fee transparency:
- All fees are displayed at checkout before purchase
- No hidden charges
- Total includes item price + tax + shipping
- Currency conversion fees may apply for international orders
- Payment processor fees not added (included in price)
- Review order summary carefully before completing purchase
