# Customer Support - General FAQs

## Account Access

### Question: How do I reset my password?
**Answer:** To reset your password:
1. Go to the login page
2. Click on "Forgot Password" link
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your email for a password reset link
5. Click the link and create a new password
6. Your password should be at least 8 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

### Question: I can't access my account
**Answer:** If you're having trouble accessing your account:
- Verify you're using the correct email address
- Check if your account has been locked due to multiple failed login attempts (wait 30 minutes)
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try resetting your password
- Ensure your email account is active and accessible
- If issues persist, contact our support team with your account details

### Question: How do I recover my username?
**Answer:** To recover your username:
1. Visit the login page
2. Click "Forgot Username"
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Your username will be sent to your email within 5 minutes
5. Check spam/junk folders if you don't see it in your inbox

## Profile and Settings

### Question: How do I update my email address?
**Answer:** To update your email:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Profile Settings" or "Account Settings"
3. Click "Edit" next to your email address
4. Enter your new email address
5. Verify the new email by clicking the confirmation link sent to it
6. Your email will be updated after verification

### Question: How do I change my notification preferences?
**Answer:** To manage notifications:
1. Go to "Settings" in your account
2. Select "Notifications" or "Communication Preferences"
3. Choose which notifications you want to receive (email, SMS, push)
4. Save your preferences
5. Changes take effect immediately

### Question: How do I delete my account?
**Answer:** To delete your account:
1. Go to "Account Settings"
2. Scroll to "Account Management"
3. Select "Delete Account"
4. Confirm your decision (this action cannot be undone)
5. Your data will be removed within 30 days according to our privacy policy
6. Alternatively, contact support for assistance with account deletion

## Orders and Delivery

### Question: How do I track my order?
**Answer:** To track your order:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Orders" or "Order History"
3. Click on the order you want to track
4. View the tracking number and delivery status
5. Click the tracking number to see detailed shipping information
6. You can also track using the link in your shipping confirmation email

### Question: What are your shipping times?
**Answer:** Standard shipping times:
- Standard Delivery: 5-7 business days
- Express Delivery: 2-3 business days
- Next Day Delivery: 1 business day (order before 2 PM)
- International: 10-15 business days
- Processing time is usually 1-2 business days before shipping

### Question: How do I cancel my order?
**Answer:** To cancel an order:
1. Go to "Order History" in your account
2. Find the order you want to cancel
3. If the order hasn't shipped, you'll see a "Cancel Order" button
4. Click it and confirm cancellation
5. Orders already shipped cannot be canceled online - you'll need to initiate a return
6. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days

## Returns and Refunds

### Question: What is your return policy?
**Answer:** Our return policy:
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery
- Products must be unused and in original packaging
- Some items like digital products are non-returnable
- Return shipping is free for defective items
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after receiving the return
- Store credit may be offered instead of refund in some cases

### Question: How do I initiate a return?
**Answer:** To return an item:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Order History"
3. Select the order containing the item
4. Click "Return Item"
5. Choose the reason for return
6. Print the return label provided
7. Pack the item securely and attach the label
8. Drop off at any authorized shipping location

### Question: When will I receive my refund?
**Answer:** Refund timeline:
- Returns are processed within 2-3 business days of receipt
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Credit cards: 5-7 business days
- Debit cards: 7-10 business days
- PayPal: 3-5 business days
- You'll receive an email confirmation once the refund is processed
